## DiskGarden 1.0.7

- Security: DiskGarden now verifies updates with a new signing key. Installing this update switches your copy over —
  it is a required update, because versions before 1.0.7 cannot check the new signature.
- Full-disk scans keep their results in memory instead of writing them to a temporary file, and results that need
  administrator rights are passed straight back to the app rather than through a file on disk.
